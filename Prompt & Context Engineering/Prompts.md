
- Suggested

```
TASK:

Analyze my day-by-day exercise routine and evaluate it against my
available training time, equipment, and stated fitness goals.

Evaluate the current routine for:

1. Effectiveness
   - benefits of the current exercises
   - training duration
   - training frequency
   - alignment with my fitness goals

2. Muscle and fitness coverage
   - adequately trained muscle groups
   - insufficiently trained muscle groups
   - disproportionately trained muscle groups
   - missing areas of fitness

3. Recovery and rest
   - rest between sets
   - rest between exercises
   - recovery time between training sessions
   - whether the current recovery pattern is appropriate

4. Potential problems
   - inefficient exercises
   - unnecessary volume
   - insufficient volume
   - poor exercise distribution
   - potential recovery issues

5. Adjustments
   - determine whether changes are actually necessary
   - if changes are necessary, recommend practical alternatives
   - use only the equipment and resources I provide
   - consider my available training time
   - explain why each adjustment is being recommended

6. Expected benefits
   - explain the specific benefits of each recommended adjustment
   - distinguish meaningful improvements from minor changes
```
